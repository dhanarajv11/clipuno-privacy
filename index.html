Privacy Policy for Clipuno

Clipuno – Shorts & Reels Maker

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipuno – Shorts & Reels Maker ("Clipuno", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Clipuno mobile application.

In short: Clipuno is designed as an offline video editing application. Your videos, images, and audio files are processed locally on your device. Clipuno does not require you to create an account and does not upload your media files to our servers.

1. Information We Access

To provide its video editing functionality, Clipuno may access media that you explicitly select or provide to the App through the Android media selection interface.

This may include:

  • Videos you select for editing or trimming.
  • Images you select for use in your video.
  • Audio or music files you select for use in your video.
  • Video files created or exported by Clipuno.

Clipuno only uses this media to provide the editing, processing, preview, export, saving, and sharing functionality requested by you.

2. How Your Media Is Used

Clipuno processes selected media locally on your device to perform functions such as:

  • Video trimming and editing.
  • Adding backgrounds or visual elements.
  • Adding text and other supported editing options.
  • Adding selected audio or music.
  • Generating and exporting the edited video.
  • Playing and previewing videos.
  • Saving exported videos to your device.
  • Sharing exported videos using Android's sharing functionality.

Your selected media is not uploaded to a Clipuno-operated cloud service for these functions.

3. Data Collection

Clipuno does not require registration or a user account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, or account credentials through the App's core functionality.

Clipuno does not intentionally collect the contents of your videos, images, or audio files for storage on our servers.

4. Internet and Network Access

Clipuno is designed to provide its core video editing functionality locally on your device and does not require an online account or cloud storage service.

The App does not provide a Clipuno-operated service for uploading or storing your edited media online.

5. Sharing Your Content

Clipuno allows you to share exported videos using the sharing features available on your Android device.

When you choose to share a video, the sharing action is initiated by you. The destination application or service you select may process the shared content according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Clipuno does not control how third-party applications handle content after you choose to share it with them.

6. Storage of Exported Videos

Videos exported by Clipuno are saved locally on your device according to the App's export functionality and the permissions or media access granted by Android.

You remain responsible for managing and deleting files stored on your device.

7. Advertising

The current version of Clipuno covered by this Privacy Policy does not display advertisements.

8. Children's Privacy

Clipuno is a general-purpose video editing application and is not specifically directed toward children.

Clipuno does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its core functionality.

9. Third-Party Services

Clipuno may use Android system functionality and third-party software libraries to provide features such as media selection, video processing, audio playback, file handling, saving media, and sharing.

These components are used to provide the functionality of the App. Where a third-party service processes information independently of Clipuno, its own privacy policy and terms may apply.

10. Data Security

Clipuno is designed to process user media locally on the device rather than transmitting it to a Clipuno-operated cloud service.

Users should keep their Android device secure and use the device's available security features to protect locally stored files.

11. Your Choices

You control which media files you select for use with Clipuno.

You can also manage files created by Clipuno using the storage and media management features available on your Android device.

You may choose whether to share an exported video with another application or service.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Clipuno's functionality, services, or privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
